I. Presentation Content:

- Clear Structure - Technical Knowledge

(Subject to student's contribution and performance)

5pt HD

Visual aids are highly effective, professional, and well-integrated into the presentation.
Presentation is excellently structured with a logical flow; transitions are seamless.
Thorough and deep coverage of topic; arguments are insightful and highly engaging.&nbsp;

4pt D

Visual aids are effective, well-designed, and integrated into the presentation.
Well-structured presentation with a clear flow; transitions are smooth.
Strong coverage of topic; arguments are clear, well-organized, and engaging.

3pt C

Visual aids are useful and adequately integrated, with minor issues in design or use.
Presentation has a good structure but may have minor issues with flow/transitions.&nbsp;
Good coverage of topic; arguments are logical and mostly well-organized.

2pt P

Visual aids are basic or somewhat useful but may have several design/use issues.
Basic structure is present but may lack logical flow; transitions are sometimes awkward.
Adequate coverage of topic; some arguments may lack clarity/organization.

1pt F

Visual aids are poorly designed, used ineffectively, or missing.
Presentation lacks clear structure and logical flow; transitions are often confusing.
Inadequate coverage of topic; arguments are unclear or not well-organized.
