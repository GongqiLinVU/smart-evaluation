Diary is exceptionally well-organized, clear, and easy to follow; structured with detailed descriptions of tasks and team contributions.

The project demonstrates at least 80% completion with exceptional progress and development. All key functionalities are fully operational. The work exceeds expectations, showcasing innovation, thorough testing, and attention to detail. Minimal errors are present, and the project shows a strong potential for further development or real-world application.

8pt D

Regularly updated after most meetings; entries provide clear details about progress and work.

The project approaches 80% completion but demonstrates limited progress. Some key features are incomplete or lack functionality. Errors or flaws may be present, and testing or debugging is insufficient. Basic requirements are met, but further work is necessary to achieve higher standards.
